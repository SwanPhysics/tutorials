Algebra Basics: Solving Linear Equations

A self-paced refresher for College Physics

Welcome

Physics uses a small set of algebra skills over and over: getting one letter by itself in an equation. This tutorial refreshes those skills, one idea at a time.

  • It adapts to you. If you solve a type of problem correctly, you move on quickly. If one gives you trouble, you get a short explanation and more practice.
  • Time: about 5 minutes if this is all familiar, up to about 30 minutes if it's rusty. There is no time limit; work at your own pace.
  • Answers: type a number such as 4, -2, 2.5, or a fraction such as 3/4. Use a minus sign or hyphen for negatives. A calculator is fine.
  • At the end you'll get a summary of what to practice. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere; print or save the summary if you need a record.
